# Fahim Mohamed Abdullah
### ZK Research Engineer, Technical Educator & Rust Programmer

I am a Zero-Knowledge Cryptography Research Engineer and Technical Educator focused on the mathematics and implementation of proving systems. Transitioning from a background in Electro-Mechanical Engineering and smart contract security, I leverage rigorous mathematical and physical frameworks to solve blockchain's greatest challenges: Privacy and Scalability. 

Through my open-source contributions and technical education initiatives, I bridge the gap between advanced cryptographic theory and practical, production-ready Rust code.

---

### Core Areas of Focus
* **ZK Cryptography:** Designing and analyzing SNARK and STARK proving systems, zkVM, and zkEVM architectures.
* **Rust Engineering:** Developing robust, optimized cryptographic primitives and open-source tooling using libraries like Arkworks.
* **Technical Education:** Breaking down complex algebraic structures and protocols into code-level execution for developers.

### Technical Skill Set
* **Languages:** Rust, Cairo, Solidity, Advanced Mathematics & Physics
* **ZK Frameworks & DSLs:** SNARKs, STARKs, zkVMs, Noir, Cairo
* **Blockchain & DeFi:** Smart Contract Security, AMM Mathematics, Real-World Asset (RWA) Tokenization

### Featured Projects & Contributions

* **STARK-Verified Voting System:** Developed and deployed a privacy-preserving decentralized voting mechanism live on the Sepolia testnet, featuring a complete frontend interface and secure Cairo/Noir primitives.
* **Smart Contract Auditing:** Previously conducted comprehensive security audits on decentralized architectures within the Ethereum ecosystem.
